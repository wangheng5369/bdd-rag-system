## 3 Business Background & Core Logic

First, let’s start with why we have this business.

The EU has introduced the New Battery Regulation. This regulation mandates that all companies selling products in the EU must build a complete, fully traceable supply chain system. It needs to identify every single participant in the upstream supply chain.

Supply chains have one key characteristic: from the mines at the very top, all the way down to the finished products at the bottom, every material movement has a verifiable evidence trail.

As you can see on this flowchart:

- Inside a single supplier: Every company’s business has three core, consecutive stages: Procurement, Production, and Sales. These stages are not separate—they are connected through material flow.

- Between different companies: An upstream supplier’s "Sales" stage directly maps to a downstream buyer’s "Procurement" stage.

Based on this simple logic, we created a standardized data collection framework. It includes all key data fields for material movements both inside companies and between companies. When we connect all this data, we can perform cross-validation and identify every upstream supplier in the chain.

## 4 Part 2: GeeTrace product  introduction

## 5 

To meet these regulatory requirements and leverage this supply chain characteristic, we built our digital product: the GeeTrace Traceability Platform.

The core capability of GeeTrace is generating different types of visualizations: Supply Chain Graphs and Product Graphs.

A Supply Chain Graph shows all suppliers from Tier 1 all the way down to the deepest tier. Importantly, it has built-in risk identification. Any highlighted area on the graph means that supplier may have risk problems and the system will alert us.



## 6Next are Product Graphs, which have two formats:including Map View and Tree Diagram View

in map format system, you just enter a vehicle’s VIN number (Vehicle Identification Number) or a component’s SN number (Serial Number), then select the key raw materials you want to trace. The system will trace layer by layer from the complete vehicle all the way back to the original mine.

Every step of the process—mining, manufacturing, and transportation—will be shown on this map. It also marks the exact production location and coordinates of every company at every tier. If any location is a high-risk region, the system will automatically alert you.

## 7 Tree Diagram View

The second format is the tree diagram. You can use any combination of the three fields on the interface to search, and the system will instantly generate a tree-structured supply chain graph.

With this tree diagram, you can clearly see every supplier at every tier—what materials they produce, where they process them, their company names, and exact coordinates. 

Just like the map view, it also automatically identifies and marks high-risk regions.



So to summarize: our platform has three main visualization views: the Supply Chain Graph, and the Product Graph which includes both Map View and Tree Diagram View. All of them can identify supply chain risks.



## 8

 Now let’s talk about the system’s risk identification and alerting feature.

We have built all EU regulatory and business risk rules directly into the GeeTrace platform. If the system detects any anomalies—like links to high-risk regions—it will automatically send a risk alert. If regulators require it, we can also disclose all relevant traceability data.

## 9 Data Validation

Next, I want to introduce one of our most important platform features: Data Validation.

Data validation is based on the evidence verification mechanism between upstream and downstream supply chains. The platform’s built-in rules can automatically identify and flag errors in data uploaded by suppliers.

To make it easy for suppliers and ensure accuracy, we have a two-tier validation system:

1. Suppliers can check their own data quality on their login side

1. Our operations team then reviews the data again

In the supplier self-inspection step: if the data they upload is incomplete, invalid, or doesn’t meet requirements, the system will give clear prompts. Suppliers just follow the prompts to fix the issues.



## 10

 After that, our operations team does a second review to fix any inconsistencies between upstream and downstream data links.

Specifically, we check three key things:

- Whether upstream batch records （批记录）exactly match downstream requisition records

- Whether all component SN numbers for a vehicle VIN are complete and accurate

- Whether the overall material flow logic across the entire supply chain makes sense

If we detect any data abnormalities, we will contact the supplier immediately to rectify the issues.

## 11 Project Implementation Results

Now let’s look at our real-world project results.

First, the P417（four seventeen） EU-related Project. As of the end of March 2025:

- We have completed full-link data uploading and validation for over 1,507 (one thousand five hundred seven) complete vehicles

- We have successfully traced 8 substance elements and 2 core components

- We have identified 52 suppliers across the chain, 24 of which have already onboarded to the GeeTrace platform

- Our longest traceability link reaches 7 tiers deep into the supply chain

For our other ongoing EU-related projects, as of the end of April this year:

- We have completed full-link data uploading and validation for over 143,000 (one hundred forty-three thousand) complete vehicles

- We are tracing 6 core substance elements and 12 key components

- We have identified 247 suppliers across the full supply chain, 124 of which have already onboarded to GeeTrace

- Our longest traceability link now reaches 8 tiers deep

## 第一部分：业务背景与核心逻辑

首先介绍一下我们的业务背景，欧盟出台了《新电池法案》，该法案强制要求所有进入欧盟市场的企业，都要建立运营一个可追溯的供应链系统，能识别出供应链上的每一个上游参与者。

要想建立这样的系统我们需要理解供应链中存在的一个重要特征：就是从供应链最上游的矿山，到最下游的产品的过程中每一次物料的流转，都存在可验证的证据链。

大家看左边的流程图

- 在单个供应商内部：都有三个连续的核心阶段——采购、生产、销售。而这三个阶段不是孤立的，而是通过物料流串联起来的

- 在企业和企业之间，上游供应商的"销售"阶段，正好对应下游供应商的"采购"阶段。

正是基于这样的逻辑，我们制定了一套标准化的数据收集方案，包含有企业内部和企业之间的物料流转所涉及到的关键数据字段。

这样上下游企业的数据串联起来后就可以进行交叉验证，并且识别出上游供应商。

## 5第二部分：GeeTrace产品介绍

基于法规要求和供应链的特征我们建立了GeeTrace平台这样一个数字化产品。

GeeTrace平台的一个重要产出物，就是可以生成可视化图谱：供应链图谱和产品图谱。

上面显示的是供应链图谱，它可以展示出一级一直到末级的所有供应商，以及与不同链路的供应商之间的联系，而且它自带有风险识别功能，像图上显示的四级橙色供应商，就代表这个供应商存在风险问题，系统会给我们预警。



6接下来是产品图谱，它有两种直观的呈现形式：

### 第一种：地图视图

在系统中只要输入车辆的VIN码（车架号），或者某个零部件的SN号（序列号），再选择你想追溯的关键原材料，系统就会从整车开始，一层一层往上追，一直追到最原始的矿山。

在整个供应链中，从开采、制造到运输的每一个环节的信息，都会在这个地图中显示出来，并且标注有每一级企业的生产地点和经纬度坐标。如果某一地点属于高风险地区，系统也会提醒。

### 第二种：树状图视图

除了地图形式以外还有树状图形式的图谱。我们可以用界面上这三个字段，进行组合搜索，系统会生成一个树形结构的供应链图谱。

通过这个树状图，可以看到，供应链上每一级的供应商的企业名称以及他们生产的材料、加工地点、精确的经纬度等。和地图视图一样，它也会自动识别并标注出高风险地区。



以上就是我们平台在可视化展示上主要包含三种视图模式，供应链图谱、地图视图和树状图视图，并且每种视图下都有风险识别的功能。



接下来我们展示的是系统中的风险识别、预警功能



我们根据业务和法规的要求将相应的风险规则内置到了GeeTrace平台里。如果系统识别到供应链中存在风险地区等异常，系统就会发出风险预警。如果相关监管机构有要求，我们也可以及时披露相关的溯源数据。





## 9第三部分：核心功能——数据校验

接下来，给大家介绍的我们平台的一个核心功能：数据校验，数据校验是基于供应链上下游的证据验证机制，通过平台的内置规则来对供应商上传的数据自动识别报错。



为了方便供应商自查和数据的真实性我们设置了两个校验主体，一个是供应商自己可以在企业登录侧自查数据质量，一个是运营人员可以对供应商上传的数据进行复核。

在供应商自查环节。如果供应商上传的数据不完整、无效或者不符合要求，系统会给出提示，供应商照着提示修改就可以。

10这是我们运营端的数据校验功能，我们的运营人员会对供应商上传的数据再次核查，来解决上下游数据对接中可能出现的不一致问题。

比如：

1. 上游的批次记录，和下游的领用记录是否完全对应上

1. 每一辆整车VIN码对应的所有零部件SN号，是不是完整准确

1. 整条供应链的物料流转逻辑，是否合理

如果检测出数据异常后我们也会联系供应商进行整改。
